🐾 Paw Stamps — Press Kit

One-liner: Paw Stamps is a stamp card for good deeds — kids collect chunky paw stamps and trade them for family rewards. No ads, no accounts, nothing leaves your device.

The story (100 words)

Paw Stamps started as a weekend project by one parent for one kid: a digital version of the coffee-shop loyalty card, but for brave moments and tidy rooms. When a good deed happens, a parent unlocks the paw with a PIN and a giant red stamp slams onto the card with confetti. Fill a card of ten and the whole screen celebrates. There are no ads, no accounts, and no data collection of any kind — everything stays on the family's own device. The chunky black-and-white blob cat is original art, drawn entirely in code.

Facts

  • Price: free (family unlock planned as a one-time purchase, never subscriptions)
  • Platforms: iPhone & iPad (iOS 15+); free web version
  • Category: Kids (4+) — parental gate on all grown-up actions
  • Privacy: App Store label "Data Not Collected" — literally nothing leaves the device
  • Made by: a solo parent developer
