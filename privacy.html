🐾 Paw Stamps — Privacy Policy

Effective November 21, 2026

Paw Stamps is a stamp wallet for families, made by Snowbreak LLC (“we”, “us”). We built it to collect nothing.

No ads. No accounts. No analytics. Nothing leaves your device unless you choose to share it.

What we collect

Nothing. Paw Stamps doesn’t collect, receive, store or share personal information from children or adults.

What stays on your device

  • Everything a family puts into the app stays on the iPhone or iPad, unless a parent chooses to share a progress card (see “Sharing” below). That includes kids’ first names (typed by a parent), the drawn face picked for each kid, stamps, rewards, history, the parent code and settings.
  • The app makes no network connections of its own. There’s no server, no account and no sign-in.
  • There are no ads and no third-party code: no analytics, no tracking and no crash-reporting tools.
  • The Home Screen widget reads the stamp count on the device and shows it. It doesn’t show a name.
  • Your device’s own backups (iCloud Backup or a computer) include the app’s data, under your Apple account and Apple’s terms. We can’t see them.

What Apple handles

  • Paw Stamps Plus (optional subscription). Apple sells it through the App Store, and Apple’s App Store handles the purchase, the payment, your Apple ID and the subscription status. We never receive your name, email, payment details or purchase history. When the app opens, it asks Apple whether a subscription is active, and Apple tells it about renewals, refunds or a purchase made on another device. Prices, subscribing and restoring appear only after a grown-up opens the Plus screen and answers the grown-ups question. Manage or cancel the subscription in your Apple ID settings. See Apple’s Privacy Policy.
  • Siri and Shortcuts. “Give a stamp in Paw Stamps” opens the app’s parent code pad. Siri handles your voice under Apple’s privacy terms.
  • Forgotten code. If a parent forgets the code, iOS itself checks Face ID, Touch ID or the device passcode. The app only learns whether the check succeeded.
  • Sharing. “Share progress card” makes a picture on the device. You choose where it goes with Apple’s share sheet. We don’t receive it. Stickers sent in Messages work like any other sticker.
  • Crash reports and App Store numbers. If you’ve chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may give us anonymous crash reports and totals such as downloads. They don’t identify you, and we add no analytics of our own.

Children’s privacy

Paw Stamps collects no data, so no personal information from children of any age is gathered, processed or shared. Parent actions, like giving stamps and Settings, sit behind the parent’s own 4-digit code. Every purchase screen, subscription management, outside link and the share sheet also sit behind a separate grown-ups question: a new multiplication written out in words each time, which can’t be turned off.

Your rights (GDPR and similar laws)

Because we don’t collect or receive personal data from the app, we hold nothing about you to access, correct, export or delete. Your family’s data is on your device, and you control it completely.

If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply, and we keep them only as long as we need to help you. You can ask us to delete them at any time. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering you (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)).

If you’re in the EU or the UK, you can also contact your local data protection authority.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes all its data. You can also erase everything in the app with Settings → “Reset everything…” (behind the parent code). Resetting doesn’t cancel a Paw Stamps Plus subscription; do that in your Apple ID settings.

Changes

If a future version changes any of this, we’ll update this page and the App Store privacy label before that version ships. One example would be optional sync through your own iCloud account, still with no access by us.
