🐾 Paw Stamps — Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 2026

Paw Stamps is a digital stamp wallet for families. We built it for our own child, and we built it to collect nothing.

What data we collect: none.

Paw Stamps does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal information — from children or adults.

How it works

  • All app data (your child's first name as entered by a parent, stamp counts, reward lists, history, parent PIN) is stored only on your device.
  • The app makes no network connections. There is no server, no account, no sign-in.
  • There are no advertisements and no third-party SDKs — no analytics, no tracking, no crash reporting.
  • We cannot see your data. Nobody can — it never leaves the device.

Children's privacy (COPPA)

Because Paw Stamps collects no data whatsoever, no personal information from children under 13 (or any age) is ever gathered, processed, or shared. Actions that change family settings sit behind a parental PIN gate.

Data deletion

Deleting the app deletes all its data. You can also erase everything in-app via Settings → "Reset everything" (behind the parent PIN).

Future versions

If a future version adds optional iCloud sync, your data would sync only through your own Apple iCloud account under Apple's privacy terms — still with no access by us. Any such change will be reflected here and in the App Store privacy label before release.
